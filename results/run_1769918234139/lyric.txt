Okay, check it out-did you know the secret to being a great dancer is all in your neck, torso, and knees? Scientists filmed 19 dudes busting moves, rated by 37 women who judged avatars (so it was just pure motion, no faces or anything). Turns out, the more your head bobbed or neck twisted, and the faster your right knee zipped around, the higher the score. Honestly, it’s kind of hilarious that scientists needed to figure this out, but here we are. "Aha!" moment: It's the swaying and twisting that scores you points. "Methodology": They filmed dudes, removed their faces, and let women judge. "Primary result": The winners were the wobbly-necked, twisty-torsoed, and fast-kneed. "How the experiment worked": Scientists filmed dancing dudes and rated their moves. So next time you hit the dance floor, just think: it's not about the face, it's about the flow!
